- [x] **Measure why every improver gate fails: the environment is stationary.** From the audited per-bank rows, fixed regret (all value available to a perfectly context-conditioned policy over the best constant policy) is **+6 and +12** per 576 tasks, and a policy fitted on 11 banks beats one fitted on 1 bank by **+1** and **-3**. Banks are drawn i.i.d. from a fixed family mix, so the optimal action is a constant, one observation estimates it as well as twenty, and residual per-bank variation is unlearnable sampling noise. Larger banks would make this *worse*. See `ACTION-SPACE-STATIONARITY-2026-09-20.md`. This subsumes the action-space diagnosis: enlarging the space tenfold grew the value of the constant, not the value of conditioning.
- [x] **Find an environment that can support the test.** Family-mix heterogeneity fails (fixed regret at most 16.8) because every region specialist helps and none harms. Varying the familiar share *between* banks lifts fixed regret from **2.9** (balanced, the original design) to **38.4** (drifting), **48.3** (beta) and **83.4** (split), with a weak nearest-neighbour learner on the public share winning 95-100% of draws.
