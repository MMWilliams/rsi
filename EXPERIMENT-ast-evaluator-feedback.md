# AST candidate and evaluator feedback test

Frozen before opening fresh labels on 2026-09-17. Development inputs are the 48 AST tasks from `runs/temperature-withheld-families-20260917/`; their labels were already inspected. New instance-transfer seeds are 902288–902293, eight AST tasks per seed, generated by `random_bank` with one public example and 23 host-held examples. These seeds have not been scored in this project.

## Edits and controls

- Candidate-generation edit: V67 and V73 with cap 512 per source, merged by program. Control: the same two sources with cap 128. No family/reference/hidden field is passed to either source.
- Evaluator feedback edit: initialize the archived temperature-0.25 listwise ranker from its 31,500 verified source candidate labels, then fine-tune 60 epochs at learning rate 0.0003 and listwise temperature 0.25 on all newly generated cap-512 candidates from the 48 inspected AST tasks, labelled by the exact host interpreter on the last 15 hidden examples. No fresh target labels may enter training.
- Fixed evaluator controls on both pool sizes: old temperature 0.25; on wide pools also temperature 0.5, temperature 1.0 and E192. All select with zero live output queries. The updated ranker and old rankers see the same wide pool.

## Endpoints and interpretation

Primary candidate endpoint: change in task reachability at the wider cap, measured on paired fresh tasks. Primary evaluator endpoint: updated minus old temperature-0.25 correct selections on the **same wide pools**. Report six paired bank differences and an exact bank bootstrap 95% interval. A scoped evaluator-growth signal requires at least five positive banks and an interval lower bound above zero; no special claim follows from better source fit. Report old-cap selection separately so generation and evaluator effects cannot be conflated.

Freeze tasks, pools, model hash and choices before opening fresh labels. Score against the last 15 host-held examples. The first eight host-held examples are unused in final scoring. Report candidate and training-label counts and generation time. This is instance transfer in a synthetic AST family, not withheld-mechanism transfer or child-improver growth. A later parent/child improver test is required for recursive growth.
