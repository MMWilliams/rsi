# Contextual AST value estimation and protected child transfer

Frozen before new bank labels on 2026-09-18. The previous 12-feature ridge update did not improve its child. Source-only leave-one-bank-out development over 288 inspected tasks compared 15 models; expanded-feature RBF (penalty 1, width 1) scored 113/288 against one query per task at 100. A later **inspected** 96-task panel scored RBF 27 versus fixed 26 and basic ridge penalty 10 at 30. This selection is exploratory and does not prove transfer. Both models are frozen as candidate training rules here; no new hyperparameter search is allowed.

The common generator is V67/V73 cap 512; ranker is the replay AST specialist; query selector is top-20 entropy. Each eight-task bank has exactly eight true output queries per deployed policy. Policies allocate zero, one or two calls per task through the same exact knapsack. All task features come from public examples, frozen candidate scores and candidate-predicted probe outputs. The outcome oracle and final labels remain outside the policy's write access.

## Checkpoints and sequence

- Initial P0 uses 288 previously inspected tasks: original AST sensor 96, allocation transfer 96, full mixed AST subset 48, and first incremental admission 48. Later P1 adds the previously inspected incremental transfer 96, for 384 tasks. The additional source acquisition is 192 probe outputs plus final correctness labels, separately charged. RBF P0/P1 and basic ridge-10 P0/P1 are the two declared training rules. Ridge-10 also gets same-size label-scrambled and duplicated-history P1 controls; the original ridge-100 P0 is a legacy reference.
- Six **admission** eight-task banks: mixed seeds 902366–902368 and AST-only seeds 902369–902371. Freeze parent allocations before revealing outputs. Each arm's deployed allocation costs 48 calls. For shared child training, collect both probe outcomes on all 48 tasks (96 acquisition calls) and final correctness for zero, one and two calls. Each child applies its fixed training rule to its own source table plus these same 48 rows.
- Twelve **transfer** eight-task banks: mixed seeds 902372–902377 and AST-only seeds 902378–902383. Freeze all child allocations and fixed controls before revealing true outputs or final labels. Every deployed arm spends 96 calls. Fixed controls are one each, random two-on-four, high entropy two-on-four and high disagreement two-on-four.

For protected admission, a child rule may replace one-each only if its **parent** scored at least two more correct tasks than one-each on admission and lost no more than one of the six banks. The gate choice is frozen before transfer; raw child scores are still reported. This gate is conservative and does not rescue a poor learned allocation by using transfer labels.

Primary recursive endpoint for each training rule: P1 child minus P0 child final correct choices on transfer. Require at least eight positive paired banks and an exact bank-bootstrap 95% interval lower bound above zero. For promotion, the protected P1 child must also exceed the strongest fixed control at the same 96 calls and both scrambled/duplicate controls. Report parent-on-transfer diagnostics, child-minus-parent, reachability, acquisition calls, candidate generation/executions, predicted output cells, and all bank scores. Passing would establish only scoped fixed-edit-space improver improvement on these synthetic AST tasks. The full system and withheld mechanisms remain separate tests.

Independent audit must regenerate all tasks and candidate pools, recompute predictions, ranks, frozen allocations, query paths, labels and trained weights. No new model may be selected after opening the transfer labels.
