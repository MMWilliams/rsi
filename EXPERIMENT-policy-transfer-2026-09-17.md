# Policy transfer and capability aggregation pilot

Freeze before generating the new banks. This is a small synthetic pilot, not a claim of open-ended RSI.

## Questions and task lanes

On the common semantic string-transformation lane, compare V67 and V73 alone with their union. The ranking policy has seven AST text features and four source indicators. Evolve its integer weights against development oracle labels; check admission; freeze it before scoring three independently seeded transfer banks. The candidate generator sees only visible examples. Each arm receives the same per-source candidate cap. Report both accuracy and generation time, because the union spends more compute than either member.

Use the same frozen candidate pools to evaluate the compatible top-five components V67, V70, V72 and V73 as one learned policy. V86 is the fifth component, but its encoding-body tasks have a different grammar and oracle. Keep its audited result as a separate branch of the unified policy, and never add its task count to semantic accuracy. Compare semantic representation arms V70+V72, selection/language arms V67+V73, and all four. Report oracle reachability (any generated candidate correct), selected accuracy, and family breakdown.

Explicit learning is defined operationally: a retained change in ranking weights, an admitted grammar/representation choice, or a visible-example encoding hypothesis that improves held-out outputs. Test each available component and the compatible aggregate. No measure here tests subjective awareness, general intelligence, or autonomous language acquisition.

## Frozen choices

Development/admission source seed 203211. Transfer seeds 203212, 203213, 203214. Each split has 12 tasks, cycling through six families; 8 visible and 13 hidden examples per task. Candidate cap 8 per source. Evolution has population 32, four generations, four elites, seed 67173. Initial frozen weights are the original V67 AST weights and zero source bonuses. For each arm set, select the evolved genome only if admission is at least frozen; this gate and all ties are deterministic. Transfer labels cannot alter the policy. Report per-seed counts and pooled descriptive counts, no significance claim from 36 tasks.

Candidate sets: V67, V73, V67+V73, V70+V72, and V67+V70+V72+V73. Evaluate each with frozen and selected learned ranking, plus direct source baselines. Candidate acquisition is shared across policies on a task; time and candidate counts are attributed to every source included in a set. A learned policy must improve transfer over its frozen comparator before claiming policy transfer. The union must beat the stronger single arm on transfer before claiming synergy. The all-four policy must beat V67+V73 on transfer before claiming useful aggregation. Preserve null/negative outcomes.

V86 independent evidence comes from the sealed `generic-body-evolution-v86-encoding-adaptive-20261017` archive; it is not a fresh transfer test in this protocol.
