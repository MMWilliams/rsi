# Top-three recommendation comparison

This matched component study compares V67 AST-ranking evolution, V72 generic
counterexample-guided mutation, and V73 self-editing grammar evolution. Each
arm receives the same fresh banks, visible/hidden split, population size,
generations, held-out admission rule, and final tasks. The arms remain
different candidate substrates, so results are a comparative benchmark rather
than a claim that their internal representations are interchangeable.
