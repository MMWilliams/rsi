# Opened integration analysis for the shifted zero-query evaluator

The fresh shifted-task feedback result established incremental **evaluator** growth. This note asks whether the new evaluator can be integrated across task types and whether the controller improves from feedback. Every result below is from already opened banks; the prospective comparison is frozen separately in `EXPERIMENT-zero-query-mixed-integration-transfer.md`.

## Action-space dissection

On the previously opened 192-task mixed bank (96 familiar, 96 shifted), E0 chose 31 correct programs at zero calls (17 familiar, 14 shifted). The later shifted ranker chose 48 (2 familiar, 46 shifted). An unavailable per-task oracle switching between just these two choices would reach 65. The shifted ranker fixed 34 E0 errors but caused 17 new errors; 33 fixes were on shifted tasks and 16 harms on familiar tasks. Thus the stronger evaluator creates material headroom for an improver, but universal application is unsafe. These numbers are regenerated by `zero_query_router_development.py` from audited saved tasks and candidate pools.

## Singular, combined, and routed approaches

An effect regressor, two-head outcome model and ridge effect model were trained on 288 separate tasks with paired E0/shifted-ranker outcomes. On the opened mixed target they scored 53, 54 and 54, respectively; E0 scored 31 and always-shifted 48. A public-feature task-type classifier scored 48 at threshold 0.5 before receiving mixed-task examples. With two-fold cross-fitting, adding 96 verified mixed rows per fold yielded effect 53, two-head 49, ridge 55 and task-type route 56. The task-type route gained eight over its earlier checkpoint, interval +4 to +13, but the ridge route gained only one and the direct effect regressor tied. These are development results, not protected improver growth. See `analysis/zero-query-router-development-20260918.json`.

The updated task-type route scored 56 versus scrambled added type labels at 49, but a balanced duplicate-history control scored 54. The later-minus-duplicate interval was -2 to +6. Its gain therefore cannot be attributed cleanly to new task information, and recognizing a task type is not the same as learning the effect of an evaluator edit. See `analysis/zero-query-router-controls-development-20260918.json`.

A direct combined top-100 ranker trained on 56,276 familiar and 28,472 shifted candidate labels scored only 41 on the opened mixed bank with equal source weights, or 45 with shifted labels weighted threefold. It recovered familiar choices but lost much of the shifted specialist's strength: on a separate opened 192-task shifted bank the two pooled models scored 36 and 54 versus the shifted-only ranker's 74. On a 96-task familiar bank the pooled models scored 16 and 14 versus E0's 13 and the shifted-only ranker's 2. The current mixture of experts has a better observed tradeoff than pooling labels into one model. See `analysis/pooled-zero-query-reranker-development-20260918.json`.

With the later task-type classifier, a 0.6 acceptance threshold scored 55/192 (15 familiar, 40 shifted) and had zero losing banks against E0 on the opened mixed panel. At 0.5 it scored 56 but familiar correctness fell to nine; at 0.7 it scored 53 with 16 familiar. A route using domain-level average verified effects reduced to always-shifted on that panel. The 0.6 threshold and all fresh-test gates were frozen before generating new banks. See `analysis/zero-query-route-threshold-development-20260918.json`.

## Current conclusion

The best opened integrated package is a **routed specialist**, but its routing component has not yet shown a clean feedback-driven improver gain. The prospective test measures general integration and later paired-effect controller growth separately, with scrambled and duplicate controls. A type classifier can support integration only; it cannot satisfy the improver-growth gate.
