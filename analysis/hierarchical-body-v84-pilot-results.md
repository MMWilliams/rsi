# V84 pilot: hierarchical generic body composition

## Question

Can a factorized typed grammar make deeper balanced bodies reachable without adding a named operator template?

## Result

The pilot used four development, four admission, and four final tasks on a fresh bank. It composed generic three- and five-node subexpressions into bodies up to 11 nodes. The lineage remained at the identity seed because no child received support from two development tasks. Final scores were legacy 2/4, direct grammar 4/4, generic body evolution 2/4, and generic lineage 2/4.

The deeper search itself reached 11-node bodies in the probe, so the capacity path is live; this four-task pilot is underpowered for transfer inference.

## Verification and limits

The independent audit passed, replaying 48 arm-task rows and checking 24 sealed artifacts. The support gate, small bank, typed instruction vocabulary, constant bounds, and hierarchical search remain authored. Archive: `runs/generic-body-evolution-v84-hierarchical-pilot-20261011`.
