Title: A Seed Agent That Improves Its Own State: Guidance and Invention Across Seven Areas, with a Ten-Seed Replication and Preregistered Ablations
Author: Maureese Williams
Primary category: cs.AI   Cross-list: cs.LG
Licence: CC BY 4.0 (choose it on arXiv's licence step); the repository's code is MIT
Comments: 30 pages, 2 figures, 13 tables. Code, runs and preregistrations: https://github.com/MMWilliams/rsi (tag seed-growth-arxiv-v2). A plain-language version is attached as an ancillary file.

Abstract (arXiv's field allows 1,920 characters):
We study a narrow form of self-improvement: can one persistent agent improve the state a fixed learning procedure works on, namely its experience, the models that guide its search, and the primitives it builds solutions from? The agent solves synthetic rule-inference tasks under a scan budget and keeps a change only when a paired interval on held-out worlds is above zero. The final protocol was frozen with file hashes, pre-stated gates and a forecast, and run on ten sealed random seeds over seven areas. On one held-out draw per seed every area and pool rose, with area medians from +32% to +431%; nine of ten seeds passed all gates. Preregistered ablations on three seeds measured each part. Without learned guidance the agent reached 6% of the full agent's growth and without invention 65%. The acceptance test did not earn its cost in growth: keeping every proposal grew as much for 29% of the scans, and spending the test's scans on experience grew more on all three seeds, so the claim that the test drives growth is withdrawn. It did keep out harmful changes: 2 of 35 kept changes hurt their own area, against 15 of 63 and 17 of 57 without it. A second evaluation draw, committed before scoring, gave the same verdict for every arm. It also bounds the growth: the starting state lacks two primitive families by design, and a start with nothing withheld reaches about a third of the full agent's growth. The agent may also change six settings of its own improver; 13 changes were adopted on ten seeds, twelve from tests in which nothing differed and one that gave a better state, unreplicated. We list what is supported, withdrawn and not shown, with limitations from an audit of the repository.
